MATH & ALGORITHMIC TRANSPARENCY
Dynamic Precision Irrigation Governing Equations
1. Moisture Deficit Equation
$$\Delta M_i = \max\left(0, M_{\text{target}, i} - M_{\text{current}, i}\right) \quad [\%]$$

Deficit is strictly non-negative; saturated soil requires 0 L water.

2. Sector Water Requirement
$$W_{\text{req}, i} = \Delta M_i \times K_i \quad [\text{Liters}]$$

\(K_i\) is soil-calibrated water volume (L) per 1% volumetric moisture lift.

3. Weather Gating Rule
$$\text{Status} = \begin{cases} \text{DELAY}, & \text{Rain} \ge 5\text{mm} \lor (\text{RH} > 80\% \land \text{Rain} \ge 2) \\ \text{INSTANT}, & \text{Otherwise} \end{cases}$$

Rain forecast suppresses irrigation to prevent waterlogging & run-off.

4. Deficit Water Optimization
$$\text{Alloc}_i = \min\left(W_{\text{req}, i}, W_{\text{res}} \times \frac{\omega_i}{\sum \omega_j}\right), \quad \omega_i = \Delta M_i \cdot V_{\text{soil}, i}$$

When reservoir is constrained, water is prioritized by soil drought vulnerability \(V\).
